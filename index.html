Countdown Peak

Ranked countdowns of extreme sports and nature moments, from #5 to #1.

About

Countdown Peak is a YouTube Shorts channel. Every video ranks five moments, from big waves and storms to BMX, skateboarding and trampoline tricks. Clips come from Creative Commons (CC BY) videos, and every author is credited in the video description.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@countdownpeak

Countdown Peak Uploader

Countdown Peak Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. Through the YouTube Data API it uploads and schedules videos, sets thumbnails, updates titles and descriptions, and posts comments on the Countdown Peak channel. It is not offered to other users.
